Squarespace, honestly

What Squarespace does well — and where it fights you.

Every platform is a trade. Here is ours on Squarespace, from building and fixing a lot of them.

Strengths

Excellent templates and typography, reliable hosting, easy editing, good enough for portfolios, restaurants and service businesses that need to look credible fast.

Limits

Speed on a phone is middling, technical SEO control is limited, custom tools mostly aren't possible, and templates push a "photo first" layout that buries the offer.

When we'd move you

Toward hand-coded when the site becomes the business's main lead source, or toward Shopify for anything beyond a small store.

How we build on it

Conversion first. Platform second.

The platform doesn't change the job: a first screen that says what you do and what to do next, the phone number where a thumb can reach it, proof beside the offer, one clear action, and a page that loads in under three seconds. We build all of that on Squarespace — and we work around the platform's limits instead of pretending they aren't there.

When a site outgrows Squarespace, we say so and migrate it without losing rankings.
