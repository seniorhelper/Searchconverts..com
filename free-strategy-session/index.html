Costs You Compounding Ground.

Here's what nobody tells you: the businesses that survive past year five don't have better products. They don't have better luck. They out-marketed their competition when it counted — and they kept doing it consistently.

The #1 reason businesses run out of money is simple: they couldn't bring in enough new customers to cover their costs. That's not a product problem. That's not a location problem. That is a marketing problem — and marketing problems have marketing solutions.

Furthermore, the gap compounds. Every month a competitor invests in SEO, AIO, and paid ads while you don't, they're building an asset. Your absence from AI search engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity isn't neutral — it's actively handing customers to whoever shows up instead.

Consequently, the 30-minute conversation we're offering you isn't a sales call. It's a diagnostic. We'll look at exactly where your business sits on the survival curve — and show you the specific, prioritized moves that would change the trajectory.
