WordPress, honestly

What WordPress does well — and where it fights you.

Every platform is a trade. Here is ours on WordPress, from building and fixing a lot of them.

Strengths

Your team can edit it. Content and blogging are first-class. The ecosystem has a solution for nearly everything. Hosting is cheap and everywhere. For content-heavy sites with an in-house editor, it is the honest default.

Limits

Plugins accumulate and every one is a speed and security risk. Themes are generic by default. Updates break things. Real speed on a phone takes discipline most WordPress builds never get.

When we'd move you

Toward hand-coded when the site rarely changes and speed matters most (the $99/month subscribe-to-own program often wins here), or toward Shopify when the site is really a store.

How we build on it

Conversion first. Platform second.

The platform doesn't change the job: a first screen that says what you do and what to do next, the phone number where a thumb can reach it, proof beside the offer, one clear action, and a page that loads in under three seconds. We build all of that on WordPress — and we work around the platform's limits instead of pretending they aren't there.

When a site outgrows WordPress, we say so and migrate it without losing rankings.
