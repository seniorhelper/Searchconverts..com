Color converts

Color is a conversion tool. Used right.

Color does three jobs on a page: it gets attention, it tells the eye where to go, and it makes one action obvious. That's the honest science — not "blue means trust for everyone." The practical rule is simpler: one high-contrast button per screen, the same color every time, and nothing else competing with it.

Red pulls the eye and signals urgency, which is why it's the button on this site. Blue reads as steady and is why it's our second color. Yellow is the highlighter. Green means go. The mistake most sites make isn't the wrong color; it's five colors fighting for one click. The full lesson, with a live button test →
