How We Got Here

Three Decades of
Learning What Works.

The first marketing campaigns I ran in the early 1990s were newspaper ads and direct mail. No algorithms, no dashboards, no attribution windows. You either got calls or you didn't. That accountability shaped everything about how I think about marketing today.

"If your marketing agency can't tell you exactly how many new customers you got this month, they're not actually helping you grow — they're just keeping themselves employed."

Over 30 years, I've worked across nearly every industry vertical — home services, healthcare, retail, professional services, construction, e-commerce, and more. I've managed everything from $500/month local campaigns to six-figure national media buys. I've watched the industry evolve from print to digital, from keyword stuffing to E-E-A-T, from Google dominance to the emergence of AI search engines rewriting the rules entirely.

What hasn't changed? Businesses still need real customers. Phone calls. Quote requests. Foot traffic. Transactions. Everything else — the rankings, the impressions, the engagement rates — those are just instruments on the dashboard. They only matter if they're pointing toward revenue.

Search Converts and its SEO sister company Eye To Ad Media exist because most marketing agencies lost sight of that. They built around selling reports, not results. We built around results — and we'll never apologize for that.

Our Approach

The Three Pillars
of How We Work.

Everything we do flows from three foundational beliefs. These aren't marketing buzzwords — they're the operating principles that shape every client engagement, every campaign decision, and every conversation we have.

01 🎯

Customers Over Clicks

We don't celebrate impressions. We celebrate phone calls, form submissions, booked appointments, and closed deals. Every strategy we build is reverse-engineered from your revenue goal — not from what makes a pretty report.

If it doesn't drive customers, we don't prioritize it. That simple rule keeps us honest and keeps your budget working where it matters most.

02 🤝

Partner, Not Vendor

Vendors take orders. Partners push back when something isn't right, bring ideas you didn't ask for, and care enough to say "that strategy won't work for your market" even when it's uncomfortable.

We become embedded in your business — learning your margins, your seasonality, your competition, and your customers — so every decision we make is contextually smart, not generically applicable.

03 ⚙️

Systems Over Campaigns

A campaign is a burst of activity. A system is a machine that compounds. We build marketing infrastructure — interconnected SEO, content, ads, automation, and AI optimization — that gets stronger the longer it runs.

This is why our clients don't churn. They don't leave because the system becomes too valuable to abandon. That's by design.

What Drives Us

The Values We
Won't Compromise On.

These aren't words on a wall. They're commitments — to every client, every campaign, every conversation. When we say we operate with integrity and transparency, we mean it shows up in how we bill, how we communicate, and how we handle the hard moments.

🧭

Integrity

We do what we say we're going to do, when we say we're going to do it. If something isn't working, we tell you before you notice. If we make a mistake, we own it and fix it — no deflection, no spin. Your trust is not something we take for granted, and we will never sacrifice it for a short-term win.

💬

Honesty

The marketing industry runs on optimism bias. We prefer clarity. If a channel isn't right for your business, we'll say so — even if it costs us revenue. If your expectations are unrealistic, we'll have that conversation early. You'll always get a straight answer from us, not a sales pitch dressed up as strategy.

🔒

Commitment

We don't pick up accounts and hand them to junior staff to run on autopilot. When you work with Search Converts, you get a dedicated expert who is personally invested in your outcomes. Your wins are our wins. Your slow months are problems we lose sleep over too. That level of commitment is what makes the difference.

🔍

Transparency

You'll always know what we're doing, why we're doing it, and what it costs. No mystery line items. No locked dashboards that only we can read. No "trust the process" without data to back it up. We believe informed clients make better partners — and better decisions about their business.

⚡

Accountability

Results matter. If we're not moving the needle on the metrics that actually drive your business — calls, leads, conversions, revenue — we need to know and we need to act. We set clear benchmarks at the start of every engagement, measure against them honestly, and course-correct without being asked. Accountability isn't punitive — it's how progress happens.

🌱

Growth Mindset

The digital marketing landscape in 2026 looks nothing like it did in 2020, and nothing like it will in 2030. AI search engines are rewriting the rules in real time. We stay ahead of those shifts — not by chasing every trend, but by building on solid fundamentals while aggressively adopting what actually works. We invest in learning because your business depends on it.
