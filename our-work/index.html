The pattern

Nine sites. One pattern.

Look back at the cards. Every site puts the promise in the headline, not the company name. Every one has the phone or the one action above the fold. Every one puts proof — photos, reviews, a credential, a tool — beside the ask. And every one has exactly one thing it wants you to do on each screen.

That's not a style. It's the anatomy of a conversion, and it works on a roofing company, a clinic, a shopping mall and a flight simulator. It'll work on yours. Walk through it step by step →
