Shopify, honestly

What Shopify does well — and where it fights you.

Every platform is a trade. Here is ours on Shopify, from building and fixing a lot of them.

Strengths

The best checkout in the business, reliable hosting, payments and shipping solved, an app for everything, and a theme system a store owner can actually edit. For products, nothing else here comes close.

Limits

Apps pile up and slow the site; themes are generic by default; the blog and content tools are weak; deep customization means Liquid and a developer. Speed on a phone takes real work.

When we'd move you

Rarely away from Shopify for a store. Toward hand-coded only when the "store" is really a tool, a configurator or a B2B portal that Shopify keeps fighting.

How we build on it

Conversion first. Platform second.

The platform doesn't change the job: a first screen that says what you do and what to do next, the phone number where a thumb can reach it, proof beside the offer, one clear action, and a page that loads in under three seconds. We build all of that on Shopify — and we work around the platform's limits instead of pretending they aren't there.

When a site outgrows Shopify, we say so and migrate it without losing rankings. The company-side quotes for Denver businesses live at Denver Web Design; the person who designs them is at zacharytyewennstedt.com; custom-coded sites and the $99/month subscribe-to-own program are at Eye To Ad Media.
