What performance marketing is

Every channel, one scoreboard: cost per customer.

Search, social, display, streaming, email — they all report different vanity numbers. Performance marketing forces them onto one scoreboard: what did a customer cost, from this channel, this month. Then money moves to whatever is winning. It's not complicated. It just requires tracking every call and form back to its source, and the discipline to cut what isn't working.

Paid search

Google Ads and PPC for people who are looking right now. The highest intent and the highest cost per click — which is why the page has to convert.

Paid social and programmatic

Meta, display, CTV and retargeting for people who aren't looking yet. Cheaper reach, longer path, measured just as hard.

Conversion built in

Every channel lands on a page we've built to convert. Fix the page and the cost per customer drops in every channel simultaneously. That's the part most performance agencies skip.

How we run it

Fix the page. Buy the traffic. Move the money.

We start with tracking — calls, forms, chats, by source — because performance marketing without it is guessing with a budget. Then the landing page: if it converts at one percent, we fix that before spending a dollar, since every point of conversion halves the cost of everything downstream.

Then the buying: search first (intent), retargeting second (cheap), then social and programmatic for reach, with CTV where the audience justifies it. Weekly, money moves toward the channel with the best cost per customer. Monthly, you get one number that matters and a plain explanation of where the next dollar goes.
