Direct answer: interactive marketing is content a visitor actively uses — calculators, quizzes, configurators, games, 3D experiences and conversational assistants — rather than passively reads. It holds attention longer, builds trust through reciprocity, and captures intent data from every input. Search Converts is an interactive marketing agency that designs and hand-codes these experiences for businesses and wraps them in pages built to be found and to convert.

Why it works

Attention, reciprocity, memory, data.

Four reasons interactive content out-converts static pages, all well-supported: people pay attention to what they control; people who receive something real are more willing to give back; people remember what they did; and every interaction tells you what they want. None of it requires a headset. Most of it is a thumb tap on a phone.

We can almost certainly code whatever you can dream up. The question is what it should make happen — and that starts with strategy.
