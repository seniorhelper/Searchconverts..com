Wix, honestly

What Wix does well — and where it fights you.

Every platform is a trade. Here is ours on Wix, from building and fixing a lot of them.

Strengths

Easy to launch and edit, reliable hosting, decent templates, and it gets a small business online in a day. For a first site that has to exist this week, it is a fair choice.

Limits

Slower pages than hand-coded or Webflow, limited control over technical SEO, generic templates, and custom tools mostly aren't possible. Migration takes care to keep rankings.

When we'd move you

Toward hand-coded subscribe-to-own (from $99/month) once the business depends on the site, or toward Shopify when the site is really a store.

How we build on it

Conversion first. Platform second.

The platform doesn't change the job: a first screen that says what you do and what to do next, the phone number where a thumb can reach it, proof beside the offer, one clear action, and a page that loads in under three seconds. We build all of that on Wix — and we work around the platform's limits instead of pretending they aren't there.
