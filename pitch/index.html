Our WorkThe PitchBlogAbout Free Strategy Session

The pitch · nine slides · about four minutes

The whole argument, no fluff.

Arrow keys, swipe, or the buttons. It ends with a yes or a not-yet — and either one is fine.

1 · The door

A website without conversion is a store with the doors locked.

Every ad dollar, every referral, every business card sends people to it. If it doesn't make them call, all of that money was spent twice — once to bring them, once to lose them.

2 · The math

97%

of visitors to a typical business site leave without doing anything. That's not a stat about your customers. It's a stat about your door. One more point of conversion on 5,000 visits is 50 more customers a month.

3 · Three jobs

Found. Chosen. Called.

Most agencies do one and call it marketing. Being found is search, Maps and AI answers — handled by Eye To Ad Media does that. Being chosen is the site. Being called is the follow-up and the sales. We do all three, in that order.

4 · What it costs to wait

Every month the door stays locked, the customers go to whoever opened theirs.

Run the numbers on the home page. Visitors, times one percent, times what a customer is worth. That's the monthly price of "we'll get to it next quarter." It never shows up on an invoice, which is why it's the most expensive line item you have.

5 · The live work

No mockups. Click any of it.

A metro-Atlanta roofing company. A clinician-led clinic with a sales-trained assistant. A walk-in 3D mall. A 2012 bath company rebuilt with a phone-photo estimator. Our own companies run on the same methods. See it →

6 · How we work

Strategy first. Then actually starting.

A free thirty-minute conversation: what you sell, what a customer is worth, where leads come from now. Then a plan, quoted to your business — most campaigns $500–$3,000 a month, websites from $99/month subscribe-to-own. No contract until you're ready. We test everything on our own companies first.

7 · Why us

We can't roof. We can't cook. We can get you found, chosen and called.

Twenty-plus years, our own companies ranking on these methods, and a founder who ran sales teams before he ran an agency. We've been burned by agencies too. We treat your business as if it were our own — and we'll tell you when the answer is "not yet."

8 · The one question

What would an extra thirty clients a week do for your business?

No promises. But we've watched it happen, with the right strategy, for businesses that decided to open the door. Picture it honestly for a second. Then decide whether it's worth a thirty-minute conversation.

9 · Yes or not yet

If we can do all of that within your budget — are you ready to get started?
